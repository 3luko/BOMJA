BOMJA

BOMJA, pronounced BOHM-jah, is born from two words, two worlds: Nkabom, meaning unity in Twi from Ghana, and Pamoja, meaning together in Swahili.

Blended, they become BOMJA — a name that unites rhythm, people, and purpose.

Nkabom

A word in Twi, from Ghana. It means unity. A reminder that we are strongest when we move as one.

Pamoja

A word from Swahili. It means together. A word that lives in movement, rhythm, laughter, and shared moments.

BOMJA

Not just experiences. But gatherings, conversations, and celebrations of who we are together.
